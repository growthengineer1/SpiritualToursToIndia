# Spiritual Tours to India brand guidelines

## Positioning

Spiritual Tours to India is a respectful, practical planning partner for
international visitors, especially first-time travelers. The promise is clear
logistics, local context, and human support—not generic transformation claims
or guaranteed outcomes.

## Voice and vocabulary

Write warmly, plainly, and specifically. Lead with what a traveler will see,
do, need, and understand; explain unfamiliar terms on first use. Use
“journey,” “visit,” “practice,” “guide,” “local context,” and “planning help”
when accurate. Avoid exoticizing language, sweeping claims about India or its
traditions, urgency tactics, and unsupported claims about healing, safety,
credentials, rituals, prices, group sizes, or availability.

## Visual system

The homepage establishes a calm editorial contrast: warm saffron/orange for
primary actions and emphasis, amber/cream for gentle section backgrounds,
white for readable cards, charcoal/gray for body copy, and photographic dark
overlays only where needed for legible hero text. Use Lora for prominent
headings and Inter for interface and body copy. Preserve generous spacing,
simple rounded cards, and clear orange hover/focus states; do not introduce
competing decorative palettes.

Photography should show real places, people, practices, and travel contexts
with dignity. Favor locally licensed, destination-specific images over
generic “mystical India” imagery. Obtain permission where required, avoid
intrusive depictions of worship, and use accurate, useful alt text rather
than keyword strings.

## Accessibility and performance

Use semantic headings in order, descriptive links, visible keyboard focus,
keyboard-operable menus/forms, sufficient text/background contrast, and
meaningful alt text (empty `alt` only for decorative images). Do not put
essential text solely in images or rely on color alone.

Target responsive images at approximately 1600–2000 px wide for heroes
(ideally under 350 KB WebP/AVIF), 800–1200 px for content images (under
200 KB), and 600–800 px for cards (under 120 KB). Supply correctly sized
variants, explicit dimensions where possible, and lazy-load below-the-fold
images. These are performance targets, not permission to degrade an image
until its subject is unclear.

## SEO and editorial ownership

The homepage owns broad “spiritual tours to India” and first-time visitor
planning intent. Each tour, destination, and resource page must own a
distinct, specific intent; do not copy the homepage's broad title, metadata,
or lead message across child pages. Keep canonicals, internal links,
structured data, and sitemap entries aligned with the page's actual purpose.

An operator must review every factual itinerary, cultural, religious,
logistical, pricing, date, accessibility, guide, and safety claim before
publication. Where a detail cannot be verified, remove it or state the
limitation plainly. Review changes with local cultural context in mind and
correct inaccuracies promptly.
