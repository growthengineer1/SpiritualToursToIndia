Kumaon Himalaya Trek: Kainchi Dham to Nag Tibba

A paced Uttarakhand route for experienced, prepared walkers

Indicative price: $3,500 - $4,800 per person

A Kumaon-to-Garhwal Mountain Journey

This is an active journey for international travelers who want walking, changing mountain landscapes, and time to notice everyday pilgrimage culture. It begins near Kainchi Dham, a temple complex outside Nainital in Kumaon, and continues by road through the hill towns of Uttarakhand toward the Nag Tibba trek near Mussoorie.

Walking is practical rather than symbolic: expect uneven paths, weather changes, and long road transfers. Quiet sitting, journaling, or simple breath awareness may be offered, but this is not a medical or therapeutic programme.

Indicative Route Plan

Days 1-2: Nainital & Kainchi Dham

Arrive, adjust to hill travel, and visit Kainchi Dham respectfully; temple hours and access are subject to local conditions.

Days 3-5: Kumaon Roads & Walks

Drive between Kumaon settlements with short acclimatisation walks and time for local meals and markets.

Days 6-8: Pantwari to Nag Tibba

The usual Nag Tibba approach starts from Pantwari. Distances, overnight arrangements, and the summit attempt depend on weather and trail conditions.

Days 9-10: Descent & Departure

Return to Pantwari and continue toward Mussoorie, Dehradun, or Delhi as confirmed for the departure plan.

What's Included

  • Final inclusions, accommodation, transport, permits, and meals are supplied only in the operator’s written confirmation.
  • Bring broken-in hiking boots, layers, rain protection, a refillable bottle, and personal medication; obtain travel insurance that covers trekking.
  • International travelers should arrange the appropriate Indian visa and allow for road delays, limited connectivity, and changing mountain weather.

Fitness, safety & confirmation

Nag Tibba is a moderate-to-challenging hike with sustained uphill and downhill walking. Ask the operator for current elevation, daily distance, guide arrangements, evacuation planning, and suitability before committing. The route, day plan, and advertised price are indicative and subject to operator confirmation.
