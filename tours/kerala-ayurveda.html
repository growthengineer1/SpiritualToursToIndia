Kerala: Ayurveda & Backwaters

Ancient Medicine + Modern Wellness

$2,200 - $4,200 per person

Tour Philosophy

Kerala is Ayurveda's birthplace—where this 5,000-year-old healing system remains a living tradition. Unlike spa-style "Ayurveda" found elsewhere, Kerala offers authentic treatments by vaidyas (physicians) following classical texts.

Combined with Kerala's legendary backwaters, temple culture, and slow village rhythms, this journey provides deep restoration. Panchakarma detox removes years of accumulated toxins while gentle nature heals the spirit.

Tour Themes

Panchakarma Detox

Traditional 7-21 day cleansing programs supervised by Ayurvedic physicians.

Houseboat Journeys

Float through Kerala's famous backwaters on traditional kettuvallam boats.

Village Life

Stay with local families, learn cooking, visit coconut farms and spice gardens.

Temple Culture

Ancient Kerala temples with unique rituals and traditional arts like Kathakali.
