Rishikesh: Yoga & Ashrams

Authentic Practice in Traditional Settings

$1,800 - $3,200 per person

Tour Philosophy

Rishikesh is where yoga was born. Nestled in the Himalayan foothills beside the sacred Ganges, this is where ancient rishis (sages) first developed the practices that have now spread worldwide. Here, yoga isn't a fitness class—it's a complete spiritual science.

Our retreats immerse you in authentic ashram life: waking before dawn for meditation, practicing asanas with lineage-holding teachers, eating sattvic meals, and experiencing the profound peace that comes from dedicated practice in a sacred environment.

Tour Themes

Traditional Yoga Practice

Daily asana, pranayama, and meditation with teachers trained in classical lineages.

Ayurveda Healing

Personalized consultations, treatments, and dietary guidance for holistic wellness.

Adventure + Yoga

Optional white-water rafting, waterfall hikes, and Himalayan nature experiences.

Ashram Immersion

Experience traditional ashram life with satsang, kirtan, and spiritual teachings.

What's Included

  • 7-10 nights authentic ashram or riverside retreat accommodation
  • Daily yoga and meditation sessions (2-4 hours)
  • Ayurvedic consultation and treatments
  • Sattvic vegetarian meals
  • Ganga Aarti ceremony attendance
  • Optional adventure activities (rafting, hiking)
  • Delhi/Dehradun airport transfers
