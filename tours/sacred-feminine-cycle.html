Tour Philosophy

Ayurveda has understood menstrual health for thousands of years. In Kerala—home to the world's most authentic Ayurvedic practitioners—you'll learn to work WITH your cycle rather than against it.

This retreat teaches cycle-synced living: different yoga for different phases, herbal support for hormonal balance, and the spiritual understanding that the menstrual cycle mirrors the moon and nature itself.
